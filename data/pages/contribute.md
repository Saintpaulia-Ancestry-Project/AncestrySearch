# Contribute cultivars

Hybridizers and collectors can add cultivars to this database: a name, the seed and pollen parents, a description, registration details and photos. You can add as many cultivars as you like before sending.

## How it works

- Fill in the form below. Only the cultivar name is required; a cultivar may have a description with no parentage, or parentage with no description.
- Your work is saved in your browser while you type, so you can close the page and come back.
- When you are done, download the file and send it to the project together with any photos. A reviewer checks every cultivar before anything is added to the database.
- Your contact details are never published. Your name is shown as the credit.

## Tips

- Start typing a parent's name and pick it from the suggestions. If a name is used by several cultivars, the list shows the hybridizer so you can choose the right one.
- If a name is not in the database, it is still accepted and added as a parent-only name.
- Write hybridizer names as Surname, Given (for example Dates, Jimmy).
- Every photo needs a credit and your confirmation that you may share it. Photos are published under the CC BY 4.0 license, like the rest of the data.
