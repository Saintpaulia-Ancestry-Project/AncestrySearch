# Downloads

Data from this site is free to download and reuse. Unless a file says otherwise, downloads are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Please credit the Saintpaulia Ancestry Project.

Cultivar names and registration data come from the AVSA (African Violet Society of America), the international registrar of African violet cultivars.

**Content from the African Violet Magazine.** Some registration details (registration numbers and dates) and notes in these files come from the AVSA Master List supplements printed in the [African Violet Magazine](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/bibliography/146187), whose digitized issues are curated by the Biodiversity Heritage Library. The online collection of the magazine is made available under a [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) license, and that content stays under those terms: credit the source, do not use it commercially, and share anything you build from it under the same license. The rest of the data, including the parentage and sports links contributed by individuals, is licensed under CC BY 4.0 as stated above. If you are unsure which applies to a record, its Notes column shows where the information came from.
