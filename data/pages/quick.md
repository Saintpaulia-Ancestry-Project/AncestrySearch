# Quick add: one cultivar

Have one cultivar to add, with no parentage to record? Paste its entry in the format the AVSA prints in its Master List and registration lists, add one photo if you like, and send it. A reviewer checks it before anything is added to the database.

An entry looks like this:

- `Aca's Lady Jane (5037) 08/12/1982 (J. Brownlie) Semidouble pink. Plain, quilted. Semiminiature (CA 288, 1983)`

## How it is read

- The **name** comes first. The **registration number** is in parentheses right after it. Unregistered cultivars have no number, and that is fine.
- The **date** is month/day/year, as the AVSA prints it: 08/12/1982 is August 12, 1982. The page writes the date out in words so you can check it.
- The **hybridizer** is in the next parentheses. "J. Brownlie" is kept as Brownlie, J.
- The **description** follows, then the **size class** (Miniature, Semiminiature, Standard, Large, Trailer...). Parentheses at the very end, such as the printed source, are kept as a note.
- Everything that cannot be placed stays in the description. Check the "What we understood" boxes and change anything that is wrong before you send.

## Before you send

- **License:** the text and the photo are published under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, like the rest of the database. You must tick the agreement box to send.
- **Photo:** one photo is welcome. Only send a photo you took or have permission to share, and give the photographer's name as the credit. The photo is shrunk on your device and camera data such as the location is removed.
- Your name is shown as the credit. Your contact (email) is private and only used if the reviewer has a question.
- If the cultivar has parents to record, or you want to correct an existing cultivar, use the [full form](#/submit).
