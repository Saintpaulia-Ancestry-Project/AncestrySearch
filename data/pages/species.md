# Species

African violet species, grouped by the AVSA code. Saintpaulia is now classified as Streptocarpus section Saintpaulia (Nishii et al., 2015), so the names follow the corrected Latin endings (ionanthus, diplotrichus, pendulus, velutinus and so on). The code in each name, such as 5c2, shows where a plant sits: 5 is S. ionanthus, 5a to 5i are its subspecies, 5c1 and 5c2 are the varieties of subspecies ionanthus, and "cl." marks a clone, a named plant in cultivation. The old spellings still find the same record.

**Sources.** Names, codes and descriptions follow K. Barbi, African Violet Species Identification Guide (MAAVS, 2019) and the accession list of J. Smith and W. Goretsky, Gesneriads 68(1), 2018, licensed under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
